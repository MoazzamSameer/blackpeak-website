Questions?

Quick answers about the soda, the cans, and getting them to your door.

What's actually in Blackpeak? +
Carbonated water, real fruit juice and flavor, a touch of cane sugar (just 9g), and a little citric acid for snap. No artificial colors, no artificial sweeteners, nothing you can't pronounce. The full ingredient list is on each can.
Is this a healthy soda? +
Better-for-you, not a wellness gimmick. Each 330ml can is about 45–50 calories with 8–10g of sugar — a fraction of a regular soda's ~140 calories and 39g. It's sweetened with real fruit and a touch of cane sugar, with nothing artificial. We won't call it a health drink, but it's an easy upgrade from cola.
How much caffeine does it have? +
Zero. All three flavors are caffeine-free, so you can crack one open at any time of day without thinking twice.
Where do you ship? +
We ship across the continental US. Free shipping on orders over $35. International is coming — get on the fizz list and we'll let you know when your country goes live.
How fast will my order get to me? +
Most orders ship within 1 business day and arrive in 3–5 business days. You'll get a tracking link the moment your case leaves our warehouse.
Can I return it if I don't love it? +
Yes. If a flavor isn't your thing, email us within 30 days and we'll refund the case — no need to ship it back. We'd rather you give the rest to a friend than throw it out.
How does the subscription work? +
The Monthly Drop sends a variety case every month at a lower price than one-off. Pause, swap flavors, or cancel anytime from your account — no awkward emails required.
Do you carry Blackpeak in stores? +
We're in select independent retailers and growing. If your favorite store doesn't stock us yet, send them our way — or hit our wholesale page and we'll handle the introduction.
Are the cans recyclable? +
Yes. Aluminum is one of the most recyclable materials on earth and can be back on a shelf within 60 days. Rinse, recycle, repeat.
When are new flavors coming? +
We've got prototypes we're not ready to ship yet. They'll be ready when they're ready. Join the fizz list and you'll be first to know.
